Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to deliver and manage your workstation access, including: full name, email address, phone number, and account access credentials.

2. Payment Information Security

We do not store complete card numbers, CVVs, or bank PINs on our servers. All financial transactions are processed through our RBI-licensed payment gateway partner, Razorpay, adhering to PCI-DSS compliance.

3. Cloud Workstation Data

Workstations are private, isolated virtual desktop instances. Files stored during your session are isolated to your ephemeral workstation instance. We do not monitor your active code, files, or screen recordings.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is only shared with authorized infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS for compute resources, Razorpay for payment processing) strictly to fulfill the service.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

For any privacy inquiries or to request account deletion, contact our privacy team at support@clouddesk.live.
