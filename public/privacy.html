Student Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to deliver and manage your educational lab access, including: student name, academic email address, phone number, and account access credentials.

2. Payment Information Security

We do not store complete credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers. All financial transactions are processed through our licensed payment gateway partners (including Razorpay), adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance standards.

3. Educational Coding Lab Runtime Data

Coding lab environments are isolated runtime sandboxes for students to complete coursework and compile code. Workspace scratchpads are ephemeral and strictly temporary for the duration of the interactive practice pass. CloudDesk does not provide permanent cloud storage, file sharing, or backup utilities.

4. Data Sharing & Infrastructure

We do not sell, rent, or trade student data. Data is shared solely with necessary technical providers (e.g., secure cloud compute infrastructure for running compilers, Razorpay for payment processing) strictly to fulfill interactive educational services.

5. Privacy Inquiries

For inquiries regarding personal data protection or to request student profile deletion, contact us at support@clouddesk.local.
