Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Instant Service Delivery

CloudDesk workstation hourly passes are digital, cloud-based compute services delivered instantly to your account wallet upon payment confirmation.

2. Technical Failure & Service Outage Refund

If a workstation fails to provision, crashes due to server infrastructure failure, or an AWS service interruption prevents you from using your allocated hours, your hours balance will be automatically re-credited to your account, or a full refund will be processed upon request.

3. Cancellation of Unused Passes

If you purchase an hourly pass and have not utilized any computing hours, you may request a cancellation and full refund within 7 days of purchase by contacting our support team.

4. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source (UPI account, debit/credit card, or netbanking account) within 5-7 business days via Razorpay.

5. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund request, email support@clouddesk.live with your registered email, Payment ID, and order details.
