Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Instant Digital Delivery

CloudDesk educational coding lab passes are digital educational services credited instantly to your student wallet upon successful payment verification.

2. Technical Interruption & Outage Guarantee

If an interactive lab instance fails to launch, crashes due to compiler infrastructure error, or a server outage interrupts your learning session, your lab time will be immediately re-credited to your account balance, or a full refund will be processed upon request.

3. Cancellation of Unused Lab Passes

If you purchase an hourly lab pass and have not initiated any learning sessions, you may request a cancellation and 100% refund within 7 days of purchase.

4. Refund Processing Window

Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method (UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking) within 5-7 business days via our RBI-authorized payment partner, Razorpay.

5. How to Contact for Refunds

To request an educational pass refund, email support@clouddesk.local with your student email and Razorpay Payment ID.
